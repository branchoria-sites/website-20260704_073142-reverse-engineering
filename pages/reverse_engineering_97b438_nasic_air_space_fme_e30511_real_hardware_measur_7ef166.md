## Introduction

The core reason [NASIC]({{ 'nasic/' | relative_url }}) studies real foreign hardware is simple: physical systems can be measured, while public claims and intelligence estimates often cannot. When the National Air and Space Intelligence Center (NASIC) obtains access to a foreign aircraft component, missile subsystem, radar, electronic-warfare device or other air and space-related equipment, analysts can replace assumptions with direct evidence. That evidence reveals actual performance, manufacturing quality, engineering trade-offs and vulnerabilities that may be invisible from photographs, specifications or operational observations alone. NASIC publicly describes foreign materiel exploitation as a means of understanding adversary capabilities through detailed technical assessment of air, space and cyberspace systems rather than relying solely on external observation.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.nasic.af.mil/News/Article-Display/Article/1010245/acquire-assess-exploit/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: nasic.af.mil">[NASIC]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">nasic.af.mil</span><span class="citation-popover-title">acquire assess exploit</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Acquire, Assess, Exploit21 Nov 2016 — The Department of Defense&#x27;s foreign materiel acquisition and exploitation programs continue to...</span></span></span>

Within the broader effort of [reverse engineering foreign military technology]({{ 'reverse-engineering-foreign-military/' | relative_url }}), the value of captured or otherwise acquired hardware is not merely that it exists. The value lies in what can be measured, tested and verified. Those measurements often become the foundation for more accurate threat assessments, [training]({{ 'training/' | relative_url }}), operational planning and future defence development.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.nasic.af.mil/News/Article/611039/center-breaks-ground-on-fme-expansion/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: nasic.af.mil">[NASIC]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">nasic.af.mil</span><span class="citation-popover-title">NASICCenter breaks ground on FME expansion</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">June 25, 2015 — 25 Jun 2015 — The National Air and Space Intelligence Center broke ground on its new 50000 square foot addition to its fo...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: June 25, 2015</span></span></span>

## What Measurements Reveal That Documents Cannot

A foreign weapon system contains information that rarely appears in brochures, intelligence reports or official statements. Even when a country publicly advertises performance figures, those figures may reflect ideal conditions, selective testing or deliberate messaging.

Direct examination allows analysts to determine characteristics such as:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--caution" markdown="1">

* Actual radar frequencies and emission patterns.
* Component tolerances and manufacturing quality.
* Guidance-system architecture and sensor design.
* Electronic-warfare capabilities and limitations.
* Software behaviour and embedded data structures.
* Material composition and production techniques.
* Reliability margins and failure points.

</div>

A radar, for example, may be advertised as having a certain detection range. Physical testing can reveal how performance changes in adverse weather, under electronic attack, or when components operate outside laboratory conditions. Likewise, a missile seeker may appear advanced on paper but contain design compromises that reduce effectiveness in realistic combat environments.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.esd.whs.mil/Portals/54/Documents/FOID/Reading%20Room/Administration_and_Management/08-F-1748_Foreign_Materiel_Program_10-10-2006.pdf?ver=2017-05-15-135556-097" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: whs.mil">[whs.mil]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">whs.mil</span><span class="citation-popover-title">08 F 1748 Foreign Materiel Program</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">WHS ESDDIRECTIVENovember 9, 2015 — 10 Oct 2006 — (U) All activities of the DoD Components that acquire and exploit foreign military and c...</span></span></span>

This distinction between claimed capability and measured capability is one of the central reasons foreign materiel exploitation remains valuable even in an era of satellites, [signals]({{ 'signals/' | relative_url }}) intelligence and extensive open-source information. Physical hardware provides a level of technical certainty that remote collection methods cannot always achieve.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://nsarchive.gwu.edu/briefing-book/intelligence/2018-01-31/scavenging-intelligence-us-governments-secret-search-foreign-objects-during-cold-war" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: nsarchive.gwu.edu">[National Security Archive]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">nsarchive.gwu.edu</span><span class="citation-popover-title">National Security Archive The U.S</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Government&#x27;s Secret Search for Foreign Objects...31 Jan 2018 — Foreign material exploitation - the overt or covert acquisition and analy...</span></span></span>

## How Direct Testing Changes Threat Assessments

Threat assessments begin as estimates. Real hardware allows those estimates to be refined, corrected or occasionally overturned.

NASIC's foreign materiel exploitation mission exists to provide detailed intelligence on the capabilities and vulnerabilities of foreign air, space and cyberspace systems. Public descriptions of the mission repeatedly emphasise understanding not only what an adversary system can do, but also where it is limited.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.nasic.af.mil/News/Article-Display/Article/3073708/leadership-changes-hands-for-the-foreign-materiel-exploitation-squadron/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: nasic.af.mil">[NASIC]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">nasic.af.mil</span><span class="citation-popover-title">leadership changes hands for the foreign materiel exploitation squadron</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Leadership changes hands for the Foreign Materiel...24 Jun 2022 — The Foreign Materiel Exploitation is tasked with delivering uniqu...</span></span></span>

Direct testing changes assessments in several important ways.

**Performance becomes quantifiable.** Engineers can generate measured data instead of relying on modelling assumptions. Range, accuracy, sensitivity and resistance to [countermeasures]({{ 'countermeasures/' | relative_url }}) can be assessed using evidence rather than inference.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://usafunithistory.com/PDF/F-S/NATIONAL%20AIR%20AND%20SPACE%20INTELLIGENCE%20CENTER.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: usafunithistory.com">[Air Force Histories]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">usafunithistory.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">NATIONAL AIR AND SPACE INTELLIGENCE CENTER</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Air Force HistoriesNATIONAL AIR AND SPACE INTELLIGENCE CENTER10 Oct 2025 — The center uses a foreign materiel exploitation capability tha...</span></span></span>

**Weaknesses become visible.** A system may contain exploitable design flaws, manufacturing shortcuts or vulnerabilities that are impossible to identify through external observation alone. Discovering those weaknesses can influence tactics, electronic countermeasures and operational planning.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.nasic.af.mil/News/Article-Display/Article/3073708/leadership-changes-hands-for-the-foreign-materiel-exploitation-squadron/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: nasic.af.mil">[NASIC]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">nasic.af.mil</span><span class="citation-popover-title">leadership changes hands for the foreign materiel exploitation squadron</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Leadership changes hands for the Foreign Materiel...24 Jun 2022 — The Foreign Materiel Exploitation is tasked with delivering uniqu...</span></span></span>

**Models become more realistic.** Military simulations and threat-representation systems depend on accurate technical data. The Department of Defense has long identified foreign materiel exploitation results as important inputs for modelling, simulation, testing, target development and training.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://media.defense.gov/1997/Oct/08/2001715489/-1/-1/1/98-005.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: media.defense.gov">[U.S. Department of War]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">media.defense.gov</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Department of WarUse of Foreign Materiel Exploitation Results8 Oct 1997 — Foreign materiel exploitation supports DoD acquisition programs...</span></span></span>

**Uncertainty is reduced.** Intelligence organisations often operate under conditions of incomplete information. Physical examination narrows uncertainty ranges and improves confidence in assessments delivered to military commanders and policymakers.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.nasic.af.mil/About-Us/Fact-Sheets/Article/611841/national-air-and-space-intelligence-center/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: nasic.af.mil">[NASIC]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">nasic.af.mil</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">National Air and Space Intelligence Center - Air ForceThe center&#x27;s team of trusted subject matter experts deliver unique collection...</span></span></span>

In practical terms, knowing that a missile has a theoretical capability is less useful than knowing precisely how that capability behaves under realistic operational conditions.

## Where Aircraft, Missiles and Radars Differ From Public Claims

Different categories of hardware present different opportunities for discovery.

### Aircraft

Aircraft reveal far more than aerodynamic shape. Internal systems expose manufacturing techniques, avionics integration, wiring architecture, electronic protection measures and [maintenance]({{ 'maintenance/' | relative_url }}) requirements. A fighter aircraft that appears sophisticated externally may contain subsystems that are less advanced than expected, or conversely may include innovations not apparent from imagery alone.

The history of NASIC's predecessor organisations illustrates this principle. During the Cold War, access to foreign aircraft allowed American analysts to move beyond speculation and directly evaluate performance characteristics, resulting in more accurate assessments of foreign aviation capabilities.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Air_and_Space_Intelligence_Center" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">National Air and Space Intelligence Center</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">National Air and Space Intelligence Center</span></span></span>

### Missiles

Missiles are particularly difficult to evaluate from external observation. Their most important features are often internal: propulsion systems, guidance electronics, seeker technologies and control algorithms.

Recovered or acquired missile hardware can reveal whether advertised accuracy is realistic, how resistant the system is to interference, and what operational limitations may exist. These findings directly affect both defensive planning and the development of countermeasures.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.nasic.af.mil/News/Article-Display/Article/1010245/acquire-assess-exploit/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: nasic.af.mil">[af.mil]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">nasic.af.mil</span><span class="citation-popover-title">acquire assess exploit</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Acquire, Assess, Exploit21 Nov 2016 — The Department of Defense&#x27;s foreign materiel acquisition and exploitation programs continue to...</span></span></span>

### Radars and Electronic Systems

Radar and electronic-warfare systems frequently produce the largest gap between appearance and reality. The external structure may reveal little about signal processing, transmitter design or electronic performance.

Physical access enables analysts to characterise actual operating parameters and determine how systems respond to jamming, deception or environmental stress. For air forces that depend on electronic superiority, these measurements can be more valuable than broad performance claims made by manufacturers or governments.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.nasic.af.mil/News/Article-Display/Article/1010245/acquire-assess-exploit/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: nasic.af.mil">[af.mil]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">nasic.af.mil</span><span class="citation-popover-title">acquire assess exploit</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Acquire, Assess, Exploit21 Nov 2016 — The Department of Defense&#x27;s foreign materiel acquisition and exploitation programs continue to...</span></span></span>

## Why Physical Evidence Remains Essential

Modern intelligence agencies possess powerful remote-collection capabilities, including satellites, signals interception and advanced data analysis. Yet foreign materiel exploitation continues to receive significant investment because some questions can only be answered by examining the object itself.

NASIC has expanded specialised facilities dedicated to foreign materiel exploitation, describing the mission as essential for understanding potential adversary capabilities. The centre's facilities support analysts, engineers and scientists who apply advanced engineering methods, modelling tools and technical testing to foreign systems.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.nasic.af.mil/News/Article-Display/Article/1356030/nasic-opens-new-fme-facility/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: nasic.af.mil">[NASIC]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">nasic.af.mil</span><span class="citation-popover-title">nasic opens new fme facility</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">NASIC opens new FME facility27 Oct 2017 — The National Air and Space Intelligence Center held a ribbon cutting ceremony to celebrate...</span></span></span>

The underlying logic is straightforward. Intelligence estimates are strongest when they rest on evidence. A [captured radar]({{ 'radar-lessons/' | relative_url }}) can be measured.
