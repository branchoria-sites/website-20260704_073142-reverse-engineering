## Introduction

The chips that matter most in [sanctions]({{ 'sanctions/' | relative_url }}) enforcement are often not cutting-edge processors. They are inexpensive, mass-produced commercial components that appear repeatedly inside missiles, drones, radios, navigation systems and electronic-warfare equipment. When investigators reverse engineer recovered military hardware, they frequently find ordinary microcontrollers, programmable logic devices, power-management chips, memory devices and timing components originally designed for civilian products. Their importance lies not in their individual cost but in their repeated appearance across weapon systems, which reveals supply-chain dependencies that sanctions authorities can target. Evidence from battlefield recoveries in Ukraine has consistently shown that a relatively small set of commercial semiconductor families has become strategically significant because they enable guidance, control, communications and processing functions in military systems.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.reuters.com/investigates/special-report/ukraine-crisis-russia-missiles-chips/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: reuters.com">[reuters.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">reuters.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">ukraine crisis russia missiles chips</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">As Russian missiles struck Ukraine, Western tech still flowed8 Aug 2022 — But many of the most important electronic components ins...</span></span></span>

## Why Dual-Use Parts Are Hard to Control

A common misconception is that military electronics depend mainly on specialised defence-grade chips. In practice, many modern weapons rely heavily on commercial-off-the-shelf electronics because they are cheaper, widely available and often powerful enough for military applications. A microcontroller that manages an industrial machine, a programmable chip used in telecommunications equipment, or a power-management component designed for consumer electronics can also perform critical functions in a missile or drone.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.reuters.com/investigates/special-report/ukraine-crisis-russia-missiles-chips/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: reuters.com">[Reuters]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">reuters.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">ukraine crisis russia missiles chips</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">As Russian missiles struck Ukraine, Western tech still flowed8 Aug 2022 — But many of the most important electronic components ins...</span></span></span>

This creates a difficult enforcement problem. The same component may be legitimately sold in huge volumes to manufacturers of appliances, vehicles, communications equipment or industrial controls. Export authorities therefore cannot simply ban every shipment of such devices without disrupting large segments of the civilian economy. The challenge is distinguishing ordinary commercial demand from procurement networks seeking components for military end use.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.csis.org/analysis/preventing-irans-military-reconstitution" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: csis.org">[CSIS]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">csis.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">preventing irans military reconstitution</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Preventing Iran&#x27;s Military Reconstitution23 Jun 2026 — In 2023, the European Union expanded controls on Iran to include broadly defin...</span></span></span>

The international focus on so-called Common High Priority Items reflects this reality. Governments in the United States, United Kingdom, European Union and partner countries have created lists of electronic components repeatedly identified in Russian weapon systems recovered from the battlefield. These lists prioritise categories of integrated circuits, communications electronics and supporting components because evidence shows they are consistently present in operational military equipment.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://finance.ec.europa.eu/system/files/2023-09/list-common-high-priority-items_en.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: finance.ec.europa.eu">[europa.eu]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">finance.ec.europa.eu</span><span class="citation-popover-title">list common high priority items en</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">List of Common High Priority Items (Version of February 2024)February 22, 2024 — The List of Common High Priority Items is divided...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: February 22, 2024</span></span></span>

## Which Ordinary Chips Keep Appearing

The recurring components identified in open-source investigations are notable because they are familiar commercial products rather than exotic military technologies.

**Microcontrollers.** These small computers perform control functions throughout weapons systems. They can manage flight controls, communications interfaces, power systems and subsystem coordination. Investigations of Russian missiles and drones have repeatedly identified microcontrollers from major commercial suppliers.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.reuters.com/investigates/special-report/ukraine-crisis-russia-missiles-chips/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: reuters.com">[Reuters]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">reuters.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">ukraine crisis russia missiles chips</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">As Russian missiles struck Ukraine, Western tech still flowed8 Aug 2022 — But many of the most important electronic components ins...</span></span></span>

**Field-programmable gate arrays (FPGAs).** These are programmable logic chips that can be reconfigured for different tasks. Their flexibility makes them useful in guidance, signal processing and communications systems. Xilinx devices, now part of AMD, have frequently appeared in recovered military equipment because a single FPGA can replace multiple custom circuits.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.reuters.com/investigates/special-report/ukraine-crisis-russia-missiles-chips/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: reuters.com">[Reuters]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">reuters.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">ukraine crisis russia missiles chips</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">As Russian missiles struck Ukraine, Western tech still flowed8 Aug 2022 — But many of the most important electronic components ins...</span></span></span>

**Digital signal processors and analogue components.** Missile guidance, navigation and communications systems often require processing of radio-frequency and sensor data. Commercial products from companies such as Analog Devices, Maxim Integrated and Texas Instruments have been repeatedly identified in recovered systems.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.occrp.org/en/news/western-tech-fuels-russias-missiles" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: occrp.org">[occrp.org]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">occrp.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">western tech fuels russias missiles</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Western Tech Fuels Russia&#x27;s Missiles3 Mar 2025 — Among the most frequently found components in Russian weapons were microprocessors...</span></span></span>

**Power-management integrated circuits.** These components regulate electrical power throughout a device. They attract less attention than processors but are essential for keeping complex electronics functioning. Recent examinations of [drone debris]({{ 'drone-debris/' | relative_url }}) have continued to find commercial power-management chips among the critical electronics used in military systems.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://cepa.org/article/western-chips-power-russias-war/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: cepa.org">[CEPA]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">cepa.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Western Chips Power Russia&#x27;s War</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Western Chips Power Russia&#x27;s WarMay 21, 2026 — A detailed teardown of Russia&#x27;s new Geran‑5 drone found US chips from Texas Instrument...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: May 21, 2026</span></span></span>

## How Repeated Models Reveal Dependencies

For sanctions enforcement, the key question is not whether a particular chip is advanced. It is whether the same model appears repeatedly across multiple weapons.

When investigators encounter identical microcontrollers or programmable chips in cruise missiles, loitering munitions and unmanned aircraft recovered months apart, they gain evidence of a continuing procurement requirement. A recurring component suggests that engineers have standardised designs around that device and that replacing it may require redesign, qualification testing and production adjustments. This creates a vulnerability that enforcement agencies can exploit.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.occrp.org/en/news/western-tech-fuels-russias-missiles" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: occrp.org">[occrp.org]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">occrp.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">western tech fuels russias missiles</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Western Tech Fuels Russia&#x27;s Missiles3 Mar 2025 — Among the most frequently found components in Russian weapons were microprocessors...</span></span></span>

The value of tracing increases when investigators can identify precise manufacturers, part numbers and production dates. Component markings can help determine whether a device was produced before or after sanctions were imposed, whether it originated from an authorised distribution channel and whether diversion occurred through intermediaries. [Battlefield recovery]({{ 'recovery-teams/' | relative_url }}) programmes increasingly use this information to map procurement networks rather than merely catalogue technical characteristics.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://storymaps.arcgis.com/stories/00594bef40bc4148b16dc7267172d033" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: storymaps.arcgis.com">[arcgis.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">storymaps.arcgis.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">ArcGIS StoryMapsIdentifying post-invasion components in Russian weaponsSeptember 9, 2025 — In March 2023, CAR investigators observed comp...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: September 9, 2025</span></span></span>

This explains why sanctions investigators often pay close attention to seemingly mundane electronics. A £5 microcontroller that appears in thousands of civilian products may provide more actionable intelligence than a rare military subsystem if it reveals a stable and traceable supply route.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.fincen.gov/system/files/shared/FinCEN%20and%20BIS%20Joint%20Alert%20_FINAL_508C.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: fincen.gov">[FinCEN.gov]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">fincen.gov</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Fin CEN and BIS Joint Alert FINAL 508C</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">components that. ## A Concrete Example: Texas Instruments, Xilinx and Analog Devices

Several commercial suppliers appear repeatedly in public analyses of recovered weapons. Investigations by journalists, research organisations and government bodies have documented recurring findings involving products from Texas Instruments, Xilinx, Analog Devices, Maxim Integrated and related semiconductor firms. ## How Enforcement Can Prioritise High-Risk Goods

The practical lesson from component tracing is that enforcement resources should focus on recurring dependencies rather than attempting to monitor every electronic device equally.

Several approaches have emerged:

* Prioritising integrated circuits and electronic categories repeatedly recovered from battlefield systems.
* Monitoring exports of Common High Priority Items through intermediary jurisdictions.<span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/russia-sanctions-common-high-priority-items-list/russia-sanctions-common-high-priority-items-list" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: GOV.UK">[GOV.UK]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">GOV.UK</span><span class="citation-popover-title">russia sanctions common high priority items list</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">It is composed of Western items critical to Russian weapons systems and its military development.Read more...</span></span></span>

## Why Ordinary Chips Become Military Bottlenecks

The strategic importance of common commercial chips comes from concentration rather than sophistication. Modern weapons often rely on a surprisingly narrow set of semiconductor families that perform control, communications, navigation and processing tasks.
