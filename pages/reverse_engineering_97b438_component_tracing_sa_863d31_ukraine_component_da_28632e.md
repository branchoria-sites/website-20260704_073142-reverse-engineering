## Introduction

Ukraine has turned battlefield forensics into a public [sanctions]({{ 'sanctions/' | relative_url }})-enforcement instrument by publishing open databases of foreign-made components recovered from Russian and Iranian weapons. Rather than keeping all technical findings inside intelligence channels, Ukrainian authorities have made large portions of this evidence searchable by governments, journalists, manufacturers, compliance teams and researchers. The goal is practical: if a missile, drone or electronic-warfare system contains identifiable foreign electronics, public documentation can help trace supply chains, expose sanctions-evasion routes and support export-control investigations. The best-known example is the “Components in Weapons” section of the War & Sanctions portal, which Ukraine presents as an open database of foreign-produced components found in aggressor weapons.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://war-sanctions.gur.gov.ua/en/components" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: war-sanctions.gur.gov.ua">[War &amp; Sanctions]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">war-sanctions.gur.gov.ua</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">War &amp; SanctionsForeign components in weapons25 May 2026 — Foreign components in weapons. Within the broader effort of sanctions enforcement through weapons component tracing, these databases are important because they convert [reverse engineering]({{ 'reverse-engineering-foreign-military/' | relative_url }}) results into evidence that can be reused outside the military and intelligence community. Instead of a closed technical report, recovered components become part of a searchable public record that can trigger scrutiny from multiple actors at once.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.business-humanrights.org/de/latest-news/ukraine-govt-launches-open-database-with-over-2000-foreign-components-found-in-russian-iranian-weapons-used-in-attacks-on-ukraine/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: business-humanrights.org">[Business and Human Rights Centre]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">business-humanrights.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Business and Human Rights Centre Ukraine: Govt</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">launches open database with over 2000...The database already includes more than 2,000 components used by Russia and Iran in various type...</span></span></span>

## What the Public Databases Contain

Ukraine's [component databases]({{ 'databases/' | relative_url }}) focus on foreign-made parts identified inside weapons recovered from the battlefield or from strike debris. The databases catalogue individual components, the weapon systems in which they were found, and associated technical details that help investigators identify manufacturers, model numbers and supply-chain relationships. According to the War & Sanctions portal, the database had grown by May 2026 to more than 5,800 documented components across more than 200 weapon systems.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://war-sanctions.gur.gov.ua/en/components" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: war-sanctions.gur.gov.ua">[War &amp; Sanctions]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">war-sanctions.gur.gov.ua</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">War &amp; SanctionsForeign components in weapons25 May 2026 — Foreign components in weapons. The initiative began publicly in late 2023, when Ukraine's National Agency on Corruption Prevention (NACP) launched what it described as the world's first open database of foreign components found in Russian and Iranian weapons. At launch, it contained information on more than 2,000 components recovered from drones, missiles, electronic-warfare equipment and other military systems.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://nazk.gov.ua/en/news/the-nacp-launches-the-world-s-first-open-database-with-over-2k-foreign-components-found-in-russian-and-iranian-weapons/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: nazk.gov.ua">[NABU]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">nazk.gov.ua</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">NABUThe NACP launches the world&#x27;s first open database with...7 Dec 2023 — The database currently contains information on foreign compone...</span></span></span>

## How Open Records Support Enforcement

The most distinctive aspect of Ukraine's approach is that the information is not restricted to governments. Public availability broadens the number of actors able to investigate sanctions circumvention.

### Giving governments actionable leads

Export-control agencies often need more than general allegations that foreign technology appears in Russian weapons. Public databases provide model numbers, component identities and links to specific weapon systems. This evidence can be compared with customs records, export licences, distributor networks and trade data. Ukraine has repeatedly argued that component tracing should feed directly into sanctions and export-control investigations.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.business-humanrights.org/de/latest-news/ukraine-govt-launches-open-database-with-over-2000-foreign-components-found-in-russian-iranian-weapons-used-in-attacks-on-ukraine/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: business-humanrights.org">[Business and Human Rights Centre]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">business-humanrights.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Business and Human Rights Centre Ukraine: Govt</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">launches open database with over 2000...The database already includes more than 2,000 components used by Russia and Iran in various type...</span></span></span>

The database therefore acts as a lead-generation system. When a component appears repeatedly in recovered weapons despite export restrictions, authorities gain a starting point for examining how that part reached Russia through intermediaries or re-export networks.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.iiss.org/research-paper/2025/09/tracking-the-components-of-missiles-and-uavs-used-by-russia-in-ukraine-what-lessons-for-control-regimes/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: iiss.org">[IISS]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">iiss.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Tracking the Components of Missiles and UAVs Used by...Sep 25, 2025 — Debris from weapons used by Russia in Ukraine shows a continue...</span></span></span>

### Enabling corporate due diligence

Manufacturers frequently state that they do not knowingly sell military-relevant products to Russia. Open component databases allow companies to see whether their products have appeared in recovered weapons and to investigate potential diversion routes through distributors, brokers or third-country resellers.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.business-humanrights.org/de/latest-news/ukraine-govt-launches-open-database-with-over-2000-foreign-components-found-in-russian-iranian-weapons-used-in-attacks-on-ukraine/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: business-humanrights.org">[business-humanrights.org]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">business-humanrights.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Business and Human Rights Centre Ukraine: Govt</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">launches open database with over 2000...The database already includes more than 2,000 components used by Russia and Iran in various type...</span></span></span>

This creates reputational pressure as well as compliance incentives. Once a component has been publicly identified, companies may tighten screening procedures, review distribution channels or cooperate with enforcement authorities. The database effectively gives firms visibility into misuse that they might otherwise never detect.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.business-humanrights.org/de/latest-news/ukraine-govt-launches-open-database-with-over-2000-foreign-components-found-in-russian-iranian-weapons-used-in-attacks-on-ukraine/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: business-humanrights.org">[Business and Human Rights Centre]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">business-humanrights.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Business and Human Rights Centre Ukraine: Govt</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">launches open database with over 2000...The database already includes more than 2,000 components used by Russia and Iran in various type...</span></span></span>

### Supporting journalists and independent investigators

## A Concrete Example: From Debris to Supply-Chain Mapping

The database's value becomes clearer when viewed as part of a tracing process rather than as a static catalogue.

Investigators recover a missile or drone, disassemble it and document its electronics. The identified components are then entered into the database and linked to the weapon platform. Researchers can compare those findings against other recovered systems and against external trade records. Over time, repeated component appearances help identify which foreign technologies remain important to Russian production.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ftm.eu/articles/russia-war-ukraine-european-tech-hidden-supply-chain" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ftm.eu">[Follow the Money]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ftm.eu</span><span class="citation-popover-title">russia war ukraine european tech hidden supply chain</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">This database contains a total of over 5,500...Read more...</span></span></span>

## Limits of Public Component Evidence

Public databases are powerful investigative tools, but they have important limitations.

First, a component's presence in a weapon does not automatically prove wrongdoing by its manufacturer. Many electronic parts move through authorised distributors, brokers, secondary markets and international resellers before reaching an end user. A company may have ceased direct sales to Russia while its products continue to circulate through third-country channels.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.iiss.org/research-paper/2025/09/tracking-the-components-of-missiles-and-uavs-used-by-russia-in-ukraine-what-lessons-for-control-regimes/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: iiss.org">[iiss.org]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">iiss.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Tracking the Components of Missiles and UAVs Used by...Sep 25, 2025 — Debris from weapons used by Russia in Ukraine shows a continue...</span></span></span>

## Why Publication Matters

Ukraine's decision to publish component data represents a shift in how battlefield intelligence is used.
